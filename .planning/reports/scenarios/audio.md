# JAL123 CVR audio and respectful presentation: discovery report

Nothing in the repo was edited. No audio or video was downloaded. WebFetch automatically cached two PDFs (the JTSB commentary, 3.6 MB, and an MLIT PDF, 226 KB) in the session's `tool-results/` folder. I did not read them and I deleted them. `git status` is clean.

## 1. What audio exists publicly

**No official release has ever happened.** The audio became public through a leak.
- In about July 2000, a cassette re-recording of the CVR reached the media. In August 2000, several TV stations broadcast it one after another. This is the Japanese Wikipedia section, mirrored at [Weblio](https://www.weblio.jp/content/%E3%83%9C%E3%82%A4%E3%82%B9%E3%83%AC%E3%82%B3%E3%83%BC%E3%83%80%E3%83%BC%E9%9F%B3%E5%A3%B0%E3%81%AE%E6%B5%81%E5%87%BA%E3%81%A8%E9%81%BA%E6%97%8F%E3%81%AE%E7%94%9F%E3%83%87%E3%83%BC%E3%82%BF%E9%96%8B%E7%A4%BA%E8%A6%81%E6%B1%82%E8%A8%B4%E8%A8%9F). The site [osutaka.com](https://osutaka.com/jal123-voice-recorder-leak/) states it was "not officially released by the government or JAL" and that the leaker was never publicly identified.
- **First broadcast: TBS "News no Mori", 8 Aug 2000. Then TV Asahi "News Station", 11 Aug 2000.** Sources: Mizushima Asaho's columns at [asaho.com, 2010](http://www.asaho.com/jpn/bkno/2010/0412.html) and [asaho.com, 13 Aug 2000](https://www.asaho.com/jpn/bkno/works/nhk20000813.html), which is written at the time. These are secondary sources. A Nippon TV airing on 11 Aug and a Fuji TV "The Nonfiction" airing on 9 Nov 2000 ([data-max](https://www.data-max.co.jp/article/27361), a conspiracy-leaning article) are **unverified**.
- **Context of the leak:** in 1999 the investigation commission discarded its investigation materials after the 10-year retention period, keeping microfilm and other copies. The Mainichi exposed this in August 2000 ([Weblio/ja-Wikipedia](https://www.weblio.jp/content/%E3%83%9C%E3%82%A4%E3%82%B9%E3%83%AC%E3%82%B3%E3%83%BC%E3%83%80%E3%83%BC%E9%9F%B3%E5%A3%B0%E3%81%AE%E6%B5%81%E5%87%BA%E3%81%A8%E9%81%BA%E6%97%8F%E3%81%AE%E7%94%9F%E3%83%87%E3%83%BC%E3%82%BF%E9%96%8B%E7%A4%BA%E8%A6%81%E6%B1%82%E8%A8%B4%E8%A8%9F)).
- **Later copies:** Yoneda Kenji's book 『御巣鷹の謎を追う』 (Takarajimasha, 1 Jul 2005) came with a DVD holding the CVR audio and CG ([hanmoto](https://www.hanmoto.com/bd/isbn/9784796646673)). TBS aired the drama-documentary 「ボイスレコーダー～残された声の記録～」 on 12 Aug 2005 ([review](https://lacroix.hatenablog.com/entry/20080804/p1)). YouTube and Dailymotion copies with English subtitles come from these sources.
- **Length and continuity:** the CVR is a 4-channel, 30-minute endless-loop unit. The channels carry ATC communication, crew conversation, cabin announcements and marker tones. The leaked tape is **about 32 minutes with the four channels mixed together, and has many unrecorded stretches**. It is not the raw 4-channel data, which is still not public ([Weblio/ja-Wikipedia](https://www.weblio.jp/content/%E3%83%9C%E3%82%A4%E3%82%B9%E3%83%AC%E3%82%B3%E3%83%BC%E3%83%80%E3%83%BC%E9%9F%B3%E5%A3%B0%E3%81%AE%E6%B5%81%E5%87%BA%E3%81%A8%E9%81%BA%E6%97%8F%E3%81%AE%E7%94%9F%E3%83%87%E3%83%BC%E3%82%BF%E9%96%8B%E7%A4%BA%E8%A6%81%E6%B1%82%E8%A8%B4%E8%A8%9F)). One blogger lists gaps totalling about 915 s, for example 18:40:26 to 18:46:02 ([note.com](https://note.com/illuyankas/n/n631653ba573e)). That claim is **unverified**, and some of those "gaps" may be silent stretches rather than cuts.
- **Conclusion:** circulated copies are *edited excerpts on a nominal 18:24:12 to 18:56:28 frame*, not a clean continuous record. The only reliable timing reference is the official Attachment 6.
- **Radio channel:** the circulated mix **includes the ATC radio**, because it combines all four channels, one of which is ATC ([Weblio](https://www.weblio.jp/content/%E3%83%9C%E3%82%A4%E3%82%B9%E3%83%AC%E3%82%B3%E3%83%BC%E3%83%80%E3%83%BC%E9%9F%B3%E5%A3%B0%E3%81%AE%E6%B5%81%E5%87%BA%E3%81%A8%E9%81%BA%E6%97%8F%E3%81%AE%E7%94%9F%E3%83%87%E3%83%BC%E3%82%BF%E9%96%8B%E7%A4%BA%E8%A6%81%E6%B1%82%E8%A8%B4%E8%A8%9F), [note.com](https://note.com/illuyankas/n/n631653ba573e)). How much ATC survives in each copy is **unverified**.
- **Official bodies publish only text.** The JTSB publishes the report with the verbatim CVR transcript ([report PDF link cited by FIJ](https://jtsb.mlit.go.jp/aircraft/download/62-2-JA8119-11.pdf)) and an explanatory commentary for the families, written with the help of the families' association 8・12連絡会 ([JTSB](https://jtsb.mlit.go.jp/kaisetsu/nikkou123.html)). The commentary's date, 2011, comes from the brief; the page does not show it. No JTSB, MLIT or JAL page publishes audio. The JAL Safety Promotion Center exhibits the CVR unit ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safety_Promotion_Center)). In 2009 it presented the flight with DFDR graphs, narration and "a flight crew–air traffic control (ATC) voice re-enactment", not the recording itself ([FSF AeroSafety World, Nov 2009](https://flightsafety.org/precious-lives-expanded-version/)). The centre was rebuilt in 2013, so the current exhibit may differ (**unverified**).
- **Fake versions circulate.** The Japan FactCheck Center debunked a viral video that added invented "we've been hit" subtitles over silent parts ([FIJ](https://www.factcheckcenter.jp/fact-check/affairs/baseless-jal123-fake-voice-recorder/)). Many subtitled uploads cannot be trusted.

## 2. Legal and ethical status (not legal advice)

- **Who holds the recording:** JAL holds the original. Families asked JAL for the raw data in 2018 and 2020. JAL refused because use outside official investigation is prohibited: 「公的な調査目的以外の使用は禁じられている」 ([Weblio](https://www.weblio.jp/content/%E3%83%9C%E3%82%A4%E3%82%B9%E3%83%AC%E3%82%B3%E3%83%BC%E3%83%80%E3%83%BC%E9%9F%B3%E5%A3%B0%E3%81%AE%E6%B5%81%E5%87%BA%E3%81%A8%E9%81%BA%E6%97%8F%E3%81%AE%E7%94%9F%E3%83%87%E3%83%BC%E3%82%BF%E9%96%8B%E7%A4%BA%E8%A6%81%E6%B1%82%E8%A8%B4%E8%A8%9F), [Nikkei](https://www.nikkei.com/article/DGXZQOUF138950T11C22A0000000/)). This reflects ICAO Annex 13 §5.12, which protects CVR recordings from uses other than investigation ([ICAO provisions via SKYbrary](https://skybrary.aero/sites/default/files/bookshelf/5876.pdf); the search summary only, I did not read the PDF).
- **The lawsuit failed.** Kibi Motoko (吉備素子), the widow of a passenger, sued for disclosure.
  - The Tokyo District Court rejected the claim on 13 Oct 2022 ([Nikkei](https://www.nikkei.com/article/DGXZQOUF138950T11C22A0000000/)).
  - The Tokyo High Court rejected it on 1 Jun 2023 ([Jomo](https://www.jomo-news.co.jp/articles/-/293161)).
  - The Supreme Court dismissed the appeal on 28 Mar 2024 ([Jisin](https://jisin.jp/domestic/2328631/)).
  - The plaintiff belongs to a separate "truth" group, not the main families' association.
- **Copyright:** Japanese law protects sound recordings through neighbouring rights. The "producer of a phonogram" is the person who first fixed the sounds. Protection lasts 70 years from publication, or from fixation if the recording was never published (Arts. 2(1)(vi), 96, 101; [Japanese Law Translation](https://www.japaneselawtranslation.go.jp/en/laws/view/3379)). A 1985 recording could therefore be protected until about 2055. Whether JAL counts as the "producer" of a CVR recording has never been tested in court (**unverified**). The broadcasters also hold rights in their 2000 and 2005 broadcasts and edits.
- **Private use:** Art. 30 allows copying for private use. It does not cover knowingly recording from an infringing upload (Art. 30(1)(iii), [lawzilla](https://lawzilla.jp/law/345AC0000000048?n=ln30&mode=only)). Most YouTube copies are unauthorised re-uploads of leaked or broadcast material.
- **Practical reading:** a personal, never-published app is low risk in practice. However:
  - Bundling the audio in a git repo that is pushed, or in any shared build, would be redistribution with no licence.
  - Keep the audio **out of the repo**, load it from a local path, and leave it off by default.
- **Families' views differ:**
  - Some families treat full disclosure of the facts as the true memorial, and the lawsuit shows that ([asaho.com](http://www.asaho.com/jpn/bkno/2010/0412.html)).
  - The leak cleared the captain's family of years of abuse. His daughter Yoko Takahama, a JAL flight attendant, said she felt her father protected them through the voice recorder ([Kawai, Yahoo! Expert](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/expert/articles/aee1b5421a4fb4c5f06a3aa0da1c534b68513001)).
  - Miyajima Kuniko of 8・12連絡会 says the media hurt families many times, and that accurate information is what gives them strength ([Yahoo, 2015](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/newshack/newshack/20150812_osutaka.html)).
  - In Christopher Hood's account, she turned away from footage of remains at the centre ([Hood](https://hoodcp.wordpress.com/2020/04/17/remembering-the-flight-jl123-crash-3-jal-safety-promotion-center/)).
  - I found no JAL statement on the 2000 leak (**not found**).

## 3. How respected bodies present it

- **JAL:** "On August 12, 1985, JAL123 crashed on the ridges of Mt. Osutaka, and 520 valuable lives perished." JAL "pledged that such a tragic accident would never occur again". The Safety Promotion Center opened on 24 Apr 2006 "to reconfirm the importance of flight safety and to embed… the lessons learned". JAL calls it a "Fortress of Safety" and teaches a "Three Actuals" principle: the actual place, object and people. This wording comes from search snippets of [jal.com](https://www.jal.com/en-jp/safety/center/); the page returned 403 to WebFetch.
  - The centre's three missions are to stop the memory fading, to instil safety in staff, and to hand the lessons on to the next generation ([FSF](https://flightsafety.org/precious-lives-expanded-version/)).
  - Exhibits include the bulkhead, the aft fuselage, farewell notes and watches that "stopped at 1856" ([FSF](https://flightsafety.org/precious-lives-expanded-version/)).
  - Photography is banned inside ([Hood](https://hoodcp.wordpress.com/2020/04/17/remembering-the-flight-jl123-crash-3-jal-safety-promotion-center/), [blog](https://blue-rock53.hatenablog.com/entry/2024/06/23/200000)).
  - Hood: such accidents "are human tragedies and not about the machine".
- **JTSB wording:** 「日本航空株式会社123便の御巣鷹山墜落事故（昭和60年8月12日発生）」 ([JTSB](https://jtsb.mlit.go.jp/kaisetsu/nikkou123.html)).
- **The memorial sites:**
  - The crash site is 御巣鷹の尾根 (Osutaka Ridge) on 高天原山 (Mt Takamagahara) in Ueno Village, Gunma. The village chief gave it that name at the police's request.
  - The 昇魂之碑 memorial stone was erected on 1 Aug 1986. 慰霊の園 (Irei no Sono) is about 10 km away and holds the memorial tower and an ossuary ([ja-Wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%BE%A1%E5%B7%A3%E9%B7%B9%E3%81%AE%E5%B0%BE%E6%A0%B9), [Hood](https://hoodcp.wordpress.com/2020/04/16/remembering-the-flight-jl123-crash-2-irei-no-sono/)).
  - Every 12 August, 520 candles are lit and a **moment of silence is held at 18:56** ([Aviation Wire, 2024](https://www.aviationwire.jp/archives/306030)). The ceremony article gives 524 aboard (509 passengers, 15 crew), 520 dead and 4 survivors ([same source](https://www.aviationwire.jp/archives/306030)).
  - 8・12連絡会 holds a lantern-floating ceremony on 11 August.
  - Note: the memorial stone lists 521 names, including an unborn child. Use **520**, the official figure.
- **What they avoid:** none of these sources depicts the impact. The centre conveys the end through the stopped watches and the notes (FSF). I did not verify how TV reconstructions such as *Mayday* show the end.

## 4. Guidance for our playback

1. **Content gate before play:** "This reconstructs a real accident in which 520 people died. It uses the crew's recorded words from the official record. It ends before the impact." Do not autoplay audio.
2. **Ending:**
   - Let the timeline reach 18:56:28, the end of the CVR record. Visuals and audio should fade out before the first *contact sound*. By the dossier OCR that is about 18:56:23 (**check against Attachment 6 p. ~330**). Starting the fade at the GPWS "SINK RATE" at 18:56:13 is safest.
   - Never render the ground strike, fire, wreckage or crash sound effects. Clamp the chase camera so it cannot follow the aircraft into terrain.
   - Scrubbing into the last few seconds shows a black screen and the clock only.
   - Hold about 5 s of silence, then show the memorial card.
   - No "Replay", score, achievements or playful sounds.
3. **Captions:**
   - Use the official English translation word for word. Label speakers by role as the record does: Captain (right seat, instructor), First Officer (left seat, flying), Flight Engineer, Tokyo Control / Tokyo Approach, Yokota Approach, JAL company radio, Cabin PA.
   - Mark radio calls made in Japanese after 18:31:26 as "[in Japanese]".
   - Never use captions from YouTube copies, the fabricated "farewell transcript", or conspiracy lines such as "hit" or "orange air".
   - Do not caption the "contact sound" lines.
4. **Audio:**
   - Default to captions only, which matches JTSB practice; the Center used a re-enactment.
   - If the user supplies a local file, reference it by an absolute path outside the repo and add it to `.gitignore`. Record provenance ("leaked 2000; rights unclear").
   - Sync it **per segment**, because the copies are edited composites with gaps, not one continuous track.
   - Avoid synthetic voices that imitate the real crew.
5. **Memorial card (suggested text):**
   > **Japan Air Lines Flight 123** · 12 August 1985 · Tokyo (Haneda) to Osaka (Itami) · Boeing 747SR-46 JA8119
   > At 18:56 the aircraft struck Osutaka Ridge (御巣鷹の尾根), Ueno Village, Gunma Prefecture. Of the 524 people aboard, 509 passengers and 15 crew, **520 died. Four survived.**
   > The crew fought for 32 minutes to fly an aircraft that had lost all hydraulics after its aft pressure bulkhead failed.
   > Every 12 August, families and JAL staff climb to the ridge, and a moment of silence is held at 18:56 at Irei no Sono.
   > JAL's Safety Promotion Center at Haneda (opened 2006) keeps the wreckage and the lessons of this accident so they are not forgotten.
   > *Reconstruction based on the official report of the Aircraft Accident Investigation Commission (1987).*

   Add the cause (a faulty 1978 repair of the bulkhead) only with a citation to the report.
