# RJA: Royal Jordanian, Airbus A320neo (A20N)

Survey key: `A20N RJA` (14 tracked frames, JY-RAA to JY-RAQ).
Reference folder: `data/livery-refs/RJA/` (git-ignored). The index is in `refs.json`.
Photo paths below are relative to that folder.

Coordinate conventions used in this dossier:

- **x**: metres aft of the nose tip, along the fuselage axis.
- **z**: metres above the keel line. The keel line is the lowest point of the constant-diameter centre fuselage.
- A320neo reference values: overall length L = 37.57 m, fuselage height H = 4.14 m, fuselage width 3.95 m, span 35.80 m.
- I measured all positions on two near-orthographic side photos:
  - Right side: `commons/JY-RAA_gerwing025794.jpg`. Nose tip at px 1807, tail-cone tip at px 104.6, which gives 45.3 px/m. Keel at px y 802.
  - Left side: `commons/JY-RAN_STN_210226.jpg`. Nose tip at px 134, tail-cone tip at px 1775, which gives 43.7 px/m.
- `commons/JY-RAH_gerwing025617.jpg` (right side) is the cross-check.
- Accuracy is about ±0.15 m in x and ±0.1 m in z. RAA's measured fuselage height is 4.08 m, which is 1.5 % short of the true 4.14 m. Scale z by 1.015 if you want exact type height.
- **Checker's re-measurement (verified):** an independent check re-measured the key values on the full-resolution original of the RAA photo, `commons/full/JY-RAA_gerwing025794_full.jpg` (4268 px wide). The nose tip is at px 4018 and the keel at px y 1782.8, which gives 100.85 px/m. This is the same frame as the 1920 px file (nose 1807 × 2.223 = 4017), so the reference values above are confirmed. Values marked "(corrected: ...)" below come from this file, from the other full-resolution files in `commons/full/`, and from `commons/JY-RAN_STN_210226.jpg` (the nose at px 135 and the tail cone at px 1775 are confirmed).

## 1. Identity

**Scheme:** the Royal Jordanian **2024 livery**. It is a refresh of the 1986 charcoal scheme. It was revealed during the airline's 60th anniversary: Air Data News (15 Dec 2023) reports the E195-E2 reveal as Embraer marking "the 60th anniversary of Royal Jordanian Airlines" (verified).

- **Where it first appeared:** Embraer showed it on the first E195-E2 in December 2023. Royal Jordanian unveiled it officially in January 2024. Wikipedia: "slight changes to the colour layout on the tail section, as well as the addition of the logo on the aircraft's belly" (verified: quote re-read on en.wikipedia.org 2026-09-29).
- **What it looks like:** a very dark charcoal-green fuselage. The classic gold, red and green lines are redrawn as a curved swoosh on the lower nose. Airways: "dark grey main paint with slight green highlights, as well as the gold, red and green lines of the classic livery, with a more modern curved design" (verified: quote re-read). The tail has a gold crown. The belly carries a crown-and-RJ logo (verified: `commons/JY-RAC_STN_140825.jpg`, `commons/JY-RAG_gerwing031019.jpg`).
- **Delivery:** every A320neo was delivered new in this scheme from 2025. The first frames are leased from Avolon. JY-RAD ("Jerash") was delivered on 29 May 2025 as the first A320neo (verified: Airways). **No tracked A20N wears the old 1986 scheme, so the frames are not mixed** (verified: all 25 Commons photos and all 13 planespotters thumbnails were re-opened, and every one shows the 2024 scheme). The classic scheme remains on the older A319, A320ceo and A321ceo fleet (JY-AY*). That fleet is not in this survey, and this statement was not checked.

**Exceptions:**

- **JY-RAK is an alliance-titles variant.** It keeps the same base livery, swoosh, tail and engines. Only the forward-fuselage titles change. Measurements are from `commons/JY-RAK_gerwing027832.jpg` (left side; nose px 107, tail cone px 1752, 43.8 px/m):
  - A oneworld roundel sits between the cockpit and door 1, on **both** sides: x ≈ 3.7–4.6 m, diameter ≈ 0.87 m, slightly above the window centreline (verified; right side in `ps/JY-RAK_ps1978862.jpg`). It is the shaded "sphere" version: navy centre (#403993 sampled) that lightens to lavender, with white "oneworld" text.
  - A large "oneworld" wordmark runs across the window line from x ≈ 6.1 m to 14.3 m (verified). "one" is bold and "world" is light. The x-height is ≈ 0.84 m and the ascenders ("l", "d") are ≈ 1.07 m (verified). The x-height band is centred on the window line, so the **baseline is ≈ 0.5 m below the window centreline** (corrected: was "≈ 0.2 m below the window centreline"). Its colour is very light grey: #c1c1c2–#cccccb on the large letters, against #eceaf0 for the white registration in the same photo. Use white #f4f4f4 or light silver #cfcfcf.
  - A small "member of" (≈ 0.25–0.3 m tall) sits above "one", at x ≈ 6.1–8.4 m (verified).
  - The normal gold English title moves **below** the windows at a smaller size: emblem at x ≈ 6.1 m, "ROYAL JORDANIAN" at x ≈ 6.6–10.7 m, cap height ≈ 0.4 m, baseline z ≈ 1.85 m (verified visually in `commons/JY-RAK_gerwing027832.jpg`; z was not re-measured). On the right side the gold text under "world" is the Arabic title (verified: `ps/JY-RAK_ps1978862.jpg`). So RAK, unlike the standard frames, swaps the languages between sides.
  - The large gold Arabic title aft of the over-wing exits is **not** present on RAK's left side. Evidence: `commons/JY-RAK_AIRCRAFT_Airbus_A320_271N_1b.jpg` shows plain charcoal above the windows aft of the exits (corrected: the evidence was `commons/JY-RAK_gerwing027832.jpg`, but in that photo the zone x 15.5–20 m is hidden behind the wing).
- No leased-in aircraft appear in other colours. No special liveries were found on the other 13 frames (verified).

## 3. Colours

Brand sources:

- There is no official livery paint specification in public.
- The only published brand colours are for the logo: gold ≈ Pantone 4505 C (#A6844B) and grey Cool Gray 10 C (#5F6367). They come from brandcolorcode.com, which says the values are "closest numbers based on the official color codes" and not from the RJ guideline itself (verified: page re-read 2026-09-29).
- The paint on the aircraft is glossy and probably metallic. Its apparent colour changes a lot with the light: blue-teal where it reflects the sky, olive where it reflects grass in evening light.

How I sampled:

- Median of box samples on sunlit side areas. ICC profiles were converted to sRGB; `JY-RAB` is ProPhoto.
- The Frankfurt photos by Olga Ernst have a strong cyan cast, so I excluded them as outliers.
- The registration letters are clipped to white in every photo, so they cannot be used for white balance.

## 7. Geometry facts for the model

- **Type:** A320-271N (Airbus designation), so **Pratt & Whitney PW1127G-JM** geared turbofan. The nacelle is smooth and round, with a large inlet (fan 2.06 m / 81 in, nacelle about 2.4–2.5 m outer diameter) and **no chevrons**. The core nozzle and plug are short. The fan cowl plus reverser length is about 3.7 m, measured on RAA. If the generic A320neo model has LEAP-1A chevron nozzles, remove them for RJA.
- **Wingtip:** Airbus **Sharklet**, about 2.43 m tall, blended into the wing tip. The root chord is about 1.5 m and the tip chord about 0.3 m. There is no lower winglet.
- **Measured landmarks** (RAA right side, ±0.15 m):

  | Landmark | Value |

  | Door 1 | x 4.33–5.39 m (left side 4.5–5.6 m) (verified: 4.45–5.41 m on RAA full-res) |

  | Rear door | x 28.9–29.95 m (verified: 28.9–29.9 m on RAA full-res) |

- **Wing root:** hidden behind the engine in every side photo. From type geometry it is about x 12.5 m (leading edge) to 18.6 m (trailing edge); this was not measured.
- **Cabin:** 180 seats, all economy (Airways; this matches the window count) (verified: Airways says 180 seats, all-economy, 3-3).
- **Engine type** (verified): Airways gives "Pratt & Whitney PW1000G", which is consistent with the -271N designation (PW1127G-JM).

## 8. Confidence and open questions

**High confidence** (seen on many photos of both sides):

- all 14 tracked frames wear the 2024 scheme (verified);
- the layout of the gold, red and green nose swoosh, **with the grey gap between the red and the green and the forward tip at x ≈ 3.1 m** (corrected);
- the gold belly line (≈0.11 m wide, corrected) and grey belly;
- title positions (English forward, Arabic aft on both sides) (verified);
- the small oneworld roundel aft of door 1 on the **left side only** (added);
- the fin crown, red cap and the two gold fin-root lines (verified);
- the red inlet band and crown + RJ on the nacelles (verified);
- the red sharklets with a charcoal band **on both faces** (corrected: the inner face was "unknown");
- a **light-grey tailplane** (corrected: was "a charcoal tailplane"). The lower surface is certain from six photos from below; the upper surface is medium confidence, from one sunlit sample.

**Medium confidence:**

- Exact paint hexes. The fuselage is a glossy, probably metallic, dark charcoal with a green-teal cast. The photos range from slate-blue to olive, and #2e3b40 is the central value.
- Gold and red are sampled; flag red is used for the red.
- The shape of the red fin-cap lower boundary: it is 0.25–0.43 m deep at the leading edge depending on the photo.

**Low confidence / open:**

1. Is the fin a slightly lighter, more neutral charcoal than the fuselage? It reads lighter in every photo, and Wikipedia mentions "changes to the colour layout on the tail section". I assume it is the same paint lit differently.
2. The belly grey value. Only low-sun samples exist, and they are warm-biased.
3. **Sharklet crown** (updated). The inner face of the blade is resolved: it shows the same band and stripe as the outer face and no crown (RAA full-res, RAB 3840, RAJ-(1)). Only `JY-RAC_STN_140825` shows a small gold crown on red, seen steeply from below. Where exactly that crown sits (probably the underside of the wing-tip blend) is open.
4. Belly-logo colour: dark charcoal or bronze-gold.
5. JY-RAE, JY-RAI, JY-RAL and JY-RAQ are confirmed only by small planespotters thumbnails, which show the standard scheme. JY-RAE had only 1 sample in the survey (verified: all four thumbnails re-opened).
6. JY-RAK: the exact size and position of the oneworld wordmark come from 3/4 and side views (±0.3 m). Its baseline is corrected to ≈ 0.5 m below the window centreline. The large Arabic is not present aft of the exits on the left side (`commons/JY-RAK_AIRCRAFT_Airbus_A320_271N_1b.jpg`). On the right side the Arabic is under the wordmark. It is unclear whether the right side carries the English title anywhere; only the 448 px thumbnail exists.
7. The door-1 calligraphic emblem and the RJ crown have no free artwork, so substitutes or omission are needed.
8. (Added) The colour of the oneworld wordmark on RAK: it samples very light grey (#c1c1c2–#cccccb), a little darker than the white registration. It is either white paint in shade or a light silver.
9. (Added) The flag-green shade: normalised samples range from #006b34 to #215144. The paint may be darker than #007a3d.
